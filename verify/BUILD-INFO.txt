11.13.0 — what changed over 11.12.5
-----------------------------------
The Differentiators release (market research: coding agents compete on
benchmarks and inline UX; none ship spend authority, digest-stamped proof
export, or measured proof-of-learning):

  1. Budget authority: the root envelope carries budgetUsd — signed into the
     canonical digest, attenuated like every other power (children tighten,
     never loosen), refused pre-flight when exhausted, and checked by the
     executor against REAL charged spend after every wave; no further seat is
     invoked once the cap is crossed. Runner UI exposes the cap (blank =
     uncapped).
  2. Proof dossier (src/mission/dossier.ts): one click on the Audit page
     exports a digest-stamped JSON — memories, beliefs, skills, the measured
     experiment, receipts, the live ledger — all derived at export time. Any
     edit after export breaks the digest (verifyProofDossier).
  3. Proof-of-learning inside the dossier: strategy versions, adopted score
     and note, measured-run count.

Plus the 9.8/10 review's nit: Settings says fourteen palettes. Numbering:
11.12.4 was intentionally skipped (11.12.5 followed 11.12.3); this feature
release opens 11.13.x.
