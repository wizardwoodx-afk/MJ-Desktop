11.10.5 — what the three new suites pin (Verified AI Delivery V1)
  #57 provenance     commit-bound AI-authorship statements on a REAL git merge: the
                     statement's subject digest IS the real merge-commit sha; materials
                     carry the writer harness + the deterministic identity digest
                     sha256(seatId|role|harness); the Ed25519 signature verifies and a
                     laundered subject is rejected; a simulated/refused merge yields NO
                     statement (provenance exists only for what happened).

                     not-declared); model versions honestly "not measured"; empty vault
                     → empty inventory; Markdown renders a paste-ready audit table.
  #59 retention      the deployer retention floor (Art. 26(6)-shaped): defaults to 6
                     months, only declared options accepted, satisfied-only-when-elapsed
                     semantics; the evidence pack names the floor and carries the AIBOM +
                     provenance statements + the new SOX 404 / NIST 800-218A mappings.
