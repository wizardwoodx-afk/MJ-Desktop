11.12.3 — what changed over 11.12.2
-----------------------------------
The 9.9/10 review named two remaining gaps; both are now mechanical:

  1. Human principal ENFORCED. issueRootEnvelope refuses to sign for any
     principal that does not match human:<id> (HUMAN_PRINCIPAL_RE).
     attenuate refuses to delegate from a non-human root, and
     verifyEnvelope flags hand-rolled envelopes. The claim "the root of
     every chain is a human" is now a checkable invariant, pinned by probe.

  2. Ledger matrix ENFORCED at the store boundary. enforceWrite is called by
     saveLessons (SCAR/PRECEDENT per record), saveSkills (approved records
     are RECOURSE), saveBeliefs (STANCE) and saveImprovement (RECOURSE)
     BEFORE anything persists. Agents may propose freely; they mechanically
     cannot install approved skills, adopt strategies, or write house rules.
     "MJ knows the rule" and "every write passes the rule" are now the same
     statement.

  3. Focus. FUNDING.md, the pitch deck and the product brief are removed;
     the investor-memo link is gone from the README. CHANGELOG now carries
     every release from 11.11.1 to 11.12.3.

No new subsystems. Counts unchanged: 63 live suites / 62 offline bundles.
Next meaningful proof per the review: real developers, real harness teams,
real evidence.
