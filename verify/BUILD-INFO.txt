11.14.2 — what changed over 11.14.1
-----------------------------------
The reviewer's decisive critique, faced mechanically: aggregate-only does NOT
automatically mean privacy-safe — narrow or repeated aggregate queries can
reconstruct rows.

  1. DatasetPolicy (src/mission/capability.ts), enforced BEFORE any
     computation: field policy (only declared fields aggregate), minimum
     cohort size (small aggregates are refused — they can expose
     individuals), a hard per-window query budget (refusals count too —
     probing IS the attack), and bounded precision (answers never carry more
     decimal grain than policy allows).
  2. capabilityAlign: 11 -> 18 assertions (cohort guard, off-policy field,
     budget exhaustion, window reset, rounding, cohortSize metadata).
  3. Guardrail manifest line 13; guardrailAlign 12 -> 13.
  4. ENTERPRISE-THESIS.md: tightened analogy (WhatsApp's selective-sharing
     mental model applied to enterprise computation), competitor honesty
     (the moat is the combination — capability + policy + cryptographic
     receipts + agent execution — not "data stays local"), and a section
     facing the hardest problem: aggregates can leak.
