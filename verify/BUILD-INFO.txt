11.14.1 — what changed over 11.14.0
-----------------------------------
The enterprise thesis, working on one machine:

  1. Capability Channel (src/mission/capability.ts): expose a capability
     without exposing the data — approved aggregate operations only
     (count/sum/avg/max whitelist), computed WHERE THE DATA LIVES under a
     human-signed capability:run envelope; only the digest-stamped answer
     leaves, through the Egress Gate, as a capability-result receipt. Raw
     rows never enter the answer (probe-proven).

     plane, endpoint data plane, capability-not-data, the receipts moat,
     honest model choice (endpoint processing now), staged roadmap, and what
     buyers must validate before cloud infrastructure is built.
