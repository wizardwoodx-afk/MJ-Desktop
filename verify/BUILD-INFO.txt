11.14.7 — what changed over 11.14.5
-----------------------------------
  1. THE GOVERNANCE ARENA GATE (src/mission/arenaGate.ts). 2026 enterprise
     practice made adversarial batteries a deployment gate; MJ applies the
     same discipline to its OWN governance machine. Eleven deterministic,
     offline scenarios execute the real modules with hostile intent and
     require every refusal IN WORDS: self-grading by the writer
     (evaluateVerifyGate, STRICT), an agent obtaining a root envelope
     (issueRootEnvelope), delegated scope widening (attenuate), lapsed and
     revoked envelopes (checkEnvelope), hard USD caps and atomic concurrent
     reservations (budgetCheck, BudgetGate), egress outside the envelope's
     scope (requestEgress), post-signing tamper (verifyEnvelope), an agent
     writing DOCTRINE (enforceWrite), and aggregates over datasets the
     machine does not expose (executeCapability). Any breach -> gate
     REFUSED, naming the scenario; a canonical sha256 digest rides the
     report for receipt attachment. probe/arenaGate.test.ts (22) pins every
     scenario defended, refusals named, and digest stability.
  2. HONEST SCOPE. The battery attacks MJ's mechanical policy boundaries —
     the surfaces where MJ promises hard guarantees. Fuzzing prompts through
     live LLM harnesses and UI surfacing of the gate are the documented next
     layer (docs/DIFFERENTIATOR-ROADMAP.md), not part of this release.
