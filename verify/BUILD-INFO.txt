11.14.3 — what changed over 11.14.2
-----------------------------------
The 11.14.2 review's final judgment, executed:

  1. THE TWO-MACHINE PROOF (src/mission/twoNode.ts): A owns the data, B
     requests a capability, the relay — a dumb pipe that logs everything it
     sees — coordinates, A computes locally under the full Privacy Guard,
     only the bounded result crosses through the Egress Gate with a
     receipt. probe/twoNodeAlign.test.ts inspects the relay's own log and
     proves it contains identity, request, authorization evidence and the
     receipt — and ZERO raw record values. A doctored answer is caught at
     B by digest mismatch. Honest scope: a protocol simulation, not real
     transport — the protocol proven before the infrastructure exists.
  2. DURABLE PRIVACY BUDGET: the in-memory budget (the review's biggest
     named weakness — restart resets it) is now an append-only,
     digest-chained, anchor-sealed ledger scoped to dataset + requester +
     window, re-read on every request. Restart resets nothing; doctoring
     or truncating the ledger refuses all computation, in words.
  3. FILTERED COHORTS: capability requests take equality filters
     (where: { region: "APAC" }); the minimum-cohort guard applies to the
     FILTERED subset — where reconstruction attacks actually live.
