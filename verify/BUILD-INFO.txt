11.14.8 — what changed over 11.14.7
-----------------------------------

     teamExecutor.ts). executeTeam now invokes the governance arena after

     gate REFUSED -> mission aborted in words, nothing executed, nothing
     spent. TeamRunReport.arena stamps gate + canonical digest + summary +
     total/defended/breached on every return path; TeamRunnerDeps.arenaRunner
     is the deterministic injection seam.

     autonomyRuntime.ts + TeamsPage). AutonomyRunSummary.arenaGate ->
     buildProofReceipt emits an arena.gate event (gate, digest, defended,
     total, summary) into the sealed SHA-256 chain. Summaries without the
     stamp keep the pre-11.14.8 event shape exactly.
  3. REVIEW NITS CLOSED (src/mission/arenaGate.ts). The exception
     classifier accepts only typed module-prefixed refusals
     (custody:/ledger:/capability:/egress: + refusal language). Canonical
     digest = sha256 over [id, title, outcome]; refusal notes stay in the
     report/event as human evidence (raw notes embed a process-global
     sequence counter and would break digest reproducibility — stated in
     code).

     stamp with the same digest as the standalone battery at one fixed
     clock; a REFUSED battery aborts before dispatch (seats 0, spent 0);
     the sealed receipt chain carries arena.gate with the mission digest;
     legacy summaries keep their old receipt shape.
