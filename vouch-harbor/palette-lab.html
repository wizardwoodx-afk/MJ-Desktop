What makes it feel real

Hairlines, not shadows. One signal colour used under 5% of the surface. 6–8px radii. Flat fills. Type does the hierarchy.

What makes it feel like an AI gimmick

Gradient text and buttons, backdrop-blur glass, glowing borders, emoji as icons, five competing accents, oversized rounding, floating orbs.

Why only four colours

Every other tone is derived by mixing the ink or the signal with the ground. A palette that can rot out of sync with itself is not a system.
