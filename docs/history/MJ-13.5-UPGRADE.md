## What 13.5 is

The **NATURA pass** — built *from* the new design skills, not alongside them.
MJ now ships three reusable design skills in `skills/` (spec-form
`SKILL.md`, frontmatter name + description, trigger sections) that encode
how MJ's surface is judged premium: `ui-premium-craft`, `ui-motion-language`,
`node-graph-craft`. Any agent (or MJ's own Knowledge Forge, through the
normal human-approved path) can load them. 13.5 then applies them.

## What changed for the user

Research direction (2026): *stark minimalism with warmth* — natural motion
eases into place, warmth comes from ink and material, not decoration.

1. **Warmth & depth.** Surfaces layer subtly — soft diffuse shadows instead
   of flat borders everywhere; cards and nodes lift 1px on hover with a
   deeper shadow; selection ring flashes once and settles; scrollbars
   slimmer and quieter; no new gradients, no glow-stacks.
2. **Natural motion vocabulary.** One deceleration curve
   (`cubic-bezier(0.33,1,0.68,1)`) for everything arriving; pages settle
   (fade + 10px rise + micro-scale) with cards drifting in one after
   another (≤ 4 steps); toasts settle rather than slide; buttons have a
   quiet press; the rail's active tick fades in; connected ports ring once
   as confirmation. Every effect is transform/opacity only and dies under
   `prefers-reduced-motion` (explicit kill-list).
3. **Readability polish.** Placeholder ink is muted; form controls and
   buttons share one transition voice; selection stays crisp but warm.

# MJ 13.5.1 — patch notes (appended to the 13.5 upgrade record)

The 13.5.0 review (9.85/10, product-polish 10/10) found one technical
issue: `ui-motion-language` said "Animate only transform and opacity"
while the NATURA CSS also transitions paint properties (box-shadow,
border-color, background, color, stroke). The skill documented a rule
stricter than the implementation. 13.5.1 takes the reviewer's option 1 —
the skill now says **layout motion uses only transform/opacity** while
**visual-state transitions may also animate short paint properties** —
so the documentation matches the code. No CSS changed. The fix is
probe-pinned (meridian 45 → **46/46**). With the skill aligned, the design
system is FROZEN (INK six + NATURA material/motion + the three design
skills); future work is the real-world evidence program.
