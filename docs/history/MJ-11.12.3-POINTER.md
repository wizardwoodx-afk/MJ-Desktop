## What landed (over 11.12.2)

- **Human principal ENFORCED.** `issueRootEnvelope` refuses to sign for any
  principal that does not match `human:<id>`; `attenuate` refuses to delegate
  from a non-human root; `verifyEnvelope` flags hand-rolled envelopes. "The
  root of every chain is a human" is now a checkable invariant, pinned by
  probe (`custody.ts`).
- **Ledger matrix ENFORCED at the store boundary.** `enforceWrite` is called
  by `saveLessons` (SCAR/PRECEDENT per record), `saveSkills` (approved records
  are RECOURSE), `saveBeliefs` (STANCE) and `saveImprovement` (RECOURSE)
  BEFORE anything persists (`ledger.ts`). "MJ knows the rule" and "every write
  passes the rule" are now the same statement.
- **Audit page.** A new `AuditPage` surfaces envelopes, principals and ledger
  decisions in the UI.
- **Focus.** `FUNDING.md`, the pitch deck and the product brief are removed;
  the investor-memo link is gone from the README. CHANGELOG now carries every
  release from 11.11.1 to 11.12.3. This tree is the build, not the pitch.
