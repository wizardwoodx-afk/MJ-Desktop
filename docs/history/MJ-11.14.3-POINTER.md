## What landed (over 11.14.2)

- **The Two-Machine Proof (`src/mission/twoNode.ts`).** A owns the data, B
  requests a capability, the relay — a dumb pipe that logs everything it
  sees — coordinates, A computes locally under the full Privacy Guard, only
  the bounded result crosses through the Egress Gate with a receipt.
  `twoNodeAlign` inspects the relay's own log and proves it contains
  identity, request, authorization evidence and the receipt — and ZERO raw
  record values. A doctored answer is caught at B by digest mismatch.
  Honest scope, stated in BUILD-INFO: a protocol simulation, not real
  transport — the protocol proven before the infrastructure exists.
- **Durable privacy budget.** The in-memory budget (restart resets it — the
  review's biggest named weakness) is now an append-only, digest-chained,
  anchor-sealed ledger scoped to dataset + requester + window, re-read on
  every request. Restart resets nothing; doctoring or truncating the ledger
  refuses all computation, in words.
- **Filtered cohorts.** Capability requests take equality filters
  (`where: { region: "APAC" }`); the minimum-cohort guard applies to the
  FILTERED subset.
