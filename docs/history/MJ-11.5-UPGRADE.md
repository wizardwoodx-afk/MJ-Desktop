# MJ 11.5 — THE MERIDIAN RELEASE

V11.4 audited loops and dieted the signal. V11.5 is a **feel** release with one rule:
**honest surfaces** — every surface tells the truth about what it is, who answered, and
what is editable. Four passes, each pinned by a new probe (suite #38, `probe/meridian.test.ts`).

The name: a meridian is the line everything else is measured from. This release draws
those lines — in the icon ink, in the hit targets, in the method contracts.

---

## 0. The owner rules, honoured mechanically

Two standing rules from the owner are now enforced by probe assertions, not by memory:

1. **Node details open on DOUBLE-click, not single-click.** Single click selects (and the
   marquee still works); double-click opens the Inspector. The store carries `inspectorId`
   separately from `selectedNodeId`, `openDetails(id)` sets it, and clearing the selection
   closes the details. The shortcuts overlay documents the gesture.
2. **Minimap node rects are the normal/small node-rect sizes** — 46×32 and 28×20 from
   `geometry.nodeHitRect()`, not squashed card ghosts. The minimap and the canvas now agree
   on what a node "is", geometrically.

## 1. The Meridian icon grammar

Every glyph in `src/canvas/icons.tsx` was redrawn on one grammar: a single **1.6-weight**
stroke, round caps, and a **1.7-radius ink dot** at the visual junction of each glyph
(`dot()` — currentColor, no stroke). 42 cases, 49 dots. Same names, same export — only the
ink changed. Wires got the same treatment: each carries a midpoint dot *on the bezier*
(`edgeMidpoint`, t = 0.5), not on the chord.

## 2. Node methods — the honest contract

`src/domain/nodeMethods.ts` (new) states, for every node, **what its method actually is**:
`NODE_METHODS` (in-built verbs per agent/capability — the owner's rule: methods are
in-built and NON-CHANGEABLE), `CATEGORY_METHODS` (honest fallbacks), `NODE_FIELDS`
(fold-fields: config keys that pre-date V11.5 are labelled `fold: pre-existing`; genuinely
new keys carry a one-line default hint), `composeAssignment()` (renders the assignment as
text: set values as `- label: value`, booleans as yes, newlines folded to `; `, nothing
invented), and `methodFor()` (node → method, category fallback, "Method not yet specified"
as the last resort — never silence, never a lie).

The Inspector shows the method as a **read-only contract line** (`method-contract`), hints
every config field from `NODE_FIELDS`, and previews the composed assignment in the Why
section. The role-pack agents (289 defs) fall back to the category verb — checked.

## 3. Assist, redesigned

The old panel opened with a wall of disclaimer text and answered with unlabelled blobs.
The redesign:

- **The provider is resolved and LABELLED before you type** (`asst-prov` chip) — you know
  who will answer; "offline" means offline.
- **Role rails on every message** (you / assist, with a dot) — no unattributed text.
- **Via chips** label model vs offline per message; offline drafts still say *why*.
- **Quick-start chips** (RSS / PDF / JSON-diff) replace the intro wall.
- **Three-dot typing indicator**, a footer that states the honesty contract, and
  **select-on-canvas** for inserted nodes (one click focuses the node).
- **Ctrl+J** toggles the panel (chord added to `shortcuts.ts`, documented in the overlay).

## 4. Aurora — the tenth palette

`aurora` joins the Inscribed system: deep blue-teal night ground (`#060a12`), one ice-blue
signal (`#7dd3fc`), readable ice ink (`#bfe8ff`), 12 hues, no gradients. It is in every
shared selector group (117 of them), in `THEME_IDS`, and in the Settings row — the theme
probe pins the count at ten and checks the palette's luminance honestly (dark ground,
bright ink, inverted selection).

## 5. Generous hit targets

- Wires are clickable across a **14px corridor** (`.wire-hit` stroke-width 14;
  `isPointNearPath` tests the bezier's control segment with the same radius).
- Port anchors carry a **12.5px hit halo** (`.port-anchor::after`).
- The canvas geometry module gained `distToSegment`, `isPointNearPath`, `edgeMidpoint`,
  `nodeHitRect`/`NODE_RECT` — all unit-tested in the extended `canvasGeometry` probe.

## Probes

- `probe/meridian.test.ts` — **new suite #38**: icon grammar, method contracts (fold-fields,
  composeAssignment format), the Assist redesign, the double-click and minimap owner rules.
- `probe/theme.test.ts` — grown to 44 assertions: the ten-palette set, aurora's luminance
  contract, the Meridian feel (cross-fade, breathing lamps, reduced-motion, hit targets).
- `probe/canvasGeometry.test.ts` — grown to 82: the two rect sizes, the 14px corridor
  boundary (14 hits, 15 doesn't), on-curve midpoints, the source wiring.

## Compatibility

No core logic was touched: the mission runtime, teams engine, and harness policies are
unchanged. The store gained one field (`inspectorId`) and one action (`openDetails`).
`nodeMethods.ts` is additive. The icon export signature is unchanged.
