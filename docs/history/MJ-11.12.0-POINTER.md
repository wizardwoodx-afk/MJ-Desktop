## What landed (the alignment extract)

- **World-belief store** (`mission/belief.ts`). Beliefs in five confidence
  classes — Known / Probably / Uncertain / Contradicted / Unknown. External
  claims cap at Probably. Contradictions surface in briefings. Predictions are
  explicitly labelled and never enter lessons, arm scores, or receipt facts.
- **Proof-carrying actions** (`mission/actionPacket.ts`). One signed
  pre-action packet carries intent, belief digest, plan step, prediction, risk,
  permission, rollback, and verification. The executor aborts on a refused
  packet or an irreversible action without an allowed packet — BEFORE anything
  runs or spends. Receipts embed the packet digest so outcome is bound to
  intention.
- **Causal memory** (`mission/lessons.ts`). Lessons gain causal edges
  (decision / action / observation / outcome) and a `retrieveCausal` mode
  answers "what happened when we tried X under similar conditions" with no
  similarity bleed.
- **Mosaic regime arm** (`mission/selfImprove.ts`). A fifth strategy
  dimension `mosaic` (shallow / normal / extreme) is added; the 11.11.1
  online experiment measures the extract only on attributed runs and adopts
  or retires it with numbers.
- **`probe/mosaicAlign.test.ts`** — 20 assertions pinning the two proof
  rules (R1 predictions are not evidence; R2 proof-carrying actions) plus
  causal retrieval and the mosaic rotation.

## Two rules that keep the proof architecture intact

1. **Predictions are not evidence.** SIMULATE output and any world-model claim
   is typed `prediction`; probes must pin that predictions never enter lesson
   memory, arm scores, or receipts' measured facts.
2. **Attribution survives routing.** The run report records the full regime
   trajectory (which strategy/regime governed which segment). Settlement scores
   arms only on runs with a stable, recorded regime; mixed-regime runs are
   logged as experience but score for no arm.
